Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding retreat booking, duration, therapies and consultations at Samwarthika Rivera.

1. What wellness programs are available at Samwarthika Rivera?

Samwarthika Rivera offers traditional Ayurvedic wellness experiences including Panchakarma, rejuvenation programs, relaxation therapies, seasonal Ayurveda retreats and personalised lifestyle guidance.

2. Are wellness programs personalised?

Yes. Ayurvedic doctors assess individual wellness goals, lifestyle preferences and relevant health information before recommending a suitable wellness program.

3. What is Panchakarma?

Panchakarma is a traditional Ayurvedic practice involving a structured series of procedures selected and supervised by qualified Ayurvedic practitioners. Suitability and program duration vary by individual.

4. Can I visit Samwarthika Rivera for relaxation and rejuvenation?

Yes. The retreat offers wellness experiences for guests looking to unwind, experience traditional Ayurveda and enjoy peaceful surroundings.

5. Are Ayurvedic doctors available for consultation?

Yes. Samwarthika Rivera has qualified Ayurvedic doctors who provide consultations and guide personalised wellness programs.

6. What facilities are available at the retreat?

The resort features Ayurvedic therapy rooms, comfortable guest accommodation, dining facilities and tranquil natural surroundings.

7. Where is Samwarthika Rivera located?

Samwarthika Rivera is located in Thottuva, Koovapady, Perumbavoor, Ernakulam, Kerala, India.

8. How can I enquire about a wellness retreat?

Submit the enquiry form or contact the team by phone or email to discuss available programs, accommodation and retreat arrangements.
